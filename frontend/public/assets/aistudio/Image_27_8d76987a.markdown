## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-stakes, life-critical utility posture engineered specifically for extreme, compromised conditions: flooded environments, rain-streaked cracked screens, low-battery states, and physical duress. It avoids decorative tech tropes, frantic alarmism, or consumer polish in favor of calm, decisive authority rooted in modern utilitarian public safety and Material 3 design principles.

### Key Tenets
- **Calm Authority under Crisis:** The interface must de-escalate panic. Feedback is rapid, steady, and clear.
- **Fail-Safe Legibility:** Information hierarchy is direct, stark, and unambiguous, supporting multi-script comprehension (Latin, Devanagari) under harsh sunlight or cracked glass.
- **Tactile Reliability:** Structural elements prioritize clumsy or wet finger navigation with exaggerated target boundaries and defensive spacing.
- **Zero Ambiguity:** State communication never relies on color alone. Every indicator pairs high-contrast iconography, explicit structural text labels, and semantic color coding.

## Colors

The palette is engineered to meet strict WCAG AAA contrast thresholds against off-white cement backdrops, maintaining perceptual distinction during reduced backlight conditions or screen damage.

### Roles & Semantic Tiers
- **Primary (`#005F60`):** Authoritative deep teal. Anchors primary actions, network synchronization states, and system-level confirmations without triggering visual distress.
- **Surface Canvas (`#F7F6F2`):** Warm, low-glare cement off-white that reduces eye strain in dark shelters while cutting outdoor reflection.
- **Surface Raised (`#FFFFFF`):** High-contrast crisp white cards for prioritized contextual information and interactive modules.
- **Text & Core Borders (`#1A1C1E`):** Near-black charcoal delivering maximum contrast ratios on all card and canvas surfaces.
- **Muted Text / Secondary Outlines (`#49454F`):** Legible secondary information (timestamps, signal attenuation stats, device hops).
- **Surface Outlines (`#DDE2DF`):** Structural, grounding borders that retain component boundary separation even if surface color values wash out in heavy rain or low backlight.
- **Warning / Pending (`#B36B00`):** Amber tone reserved strictly for pending synchronization, mesh delays, or flood advisory notices.
- **Critical / Abort (`#9E2A2B`):** Muted brick red strictly isolated to aborting SOS transmissions, emergency overrides, or battery-depletion warnings.
- **Verified / Safe (`#1E6B47`):** Deep forest green reserved for acknowledged messages, verified human contacts, and successful mesh relays.

## Typography

Typography uses `Noto Sans` across all display scales to guarantee seamless glyph parity, baseline alignment, and rendering clarity between English, Hindi, Marathi, and other Indic scripts during offline font loading.

### Scaling & Legibility Rules
- **Base Reading Scale:** Standard body text is elevated to a baseline of 18px (`body-lg`) to remain legible despite rain droplets distorting the screen pixels.
- **Action Dominance:** Primary interactive buttons scale at 20px (`action-primary`) bold to support split-second decision-making.
- **De-emphasized Text Floor:** No functional UI label drops below 14px. Any text below this threshold is unreadable on severely cracked or water-refracted displays.
- **Line Heights:** Generous line heights prevent vertical overcrowding when complex Devanagari vowel ligatures (matras) render adjacent to Roman numerals.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a disciplined single-column fluid mobile model prioritizing physical thumb reachability and immediate comprehension.

### Layout Principles
- **Touch Target Safeguard:** All interactable targets must span a physical minimum of 64px in vertical height, allowing wet hands and erratic finger input to trigger actions without misfires.
- **Thumb-Zone Prioritization:** Mission-critical emergency calls, relay broadcast controls, and urgent status filters anchor strictly within the lower 40% of the viewport.
- **Card Separations:** Stacked cards and status listings utilize a standard `1rem` gap with explicit outer margins (`0.75rem` on mobile, `1rem` on larger hand units) to prevent unintended double-taps on dense mesh telemetry feeds.
- **Breakpoint Dynamics:** Scales smoothly from compact 360dp Android devices up to larger rugged field tablets by bounding central content to a maximum readable width of 640dp, maintaining centered ergonomics.

## Elevation & Depth

To maximize battery life and maintain visual contrast on budget LCD screens with low contrast ratios, depth does not rely on soft-blurs, diffuse ambient drop-shadows, or complex lighting simulations.

### Surface System
- **Tier 0 (Base Canvas):** Warm off-white `#F7F6F2`, zero elevation.
- **Tier 1 (Cards, Modules, Input Containers):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surface framed by an omnipresent 1.5px solid border in `#DDE2DF`.
- **Tier 2 (Sticky App Bars, Bottom Action Trays):** Pure `#FFFFFF` anchored by a distinct 2px bottom/top separator in `#1A1C1E` (12% opacity) and a direct, functional 2dp non-blurred boundary drop (`0 2px 0 rgba(26,28,30,0.08)`).
- **Tier 3 (Urgent Sheets, Critical Dialogs):** `#FFFFFF` paired with an aggressive high-contrast `#1A1C1E` outer border (2px) over a heavy 60% opacity solid scrim (`#1A1C1E`). Soft glassmorphic effects are prohibited.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary uses structured, predictable curvature to balance structural toughness with touch comfort.

### Radius System
- **Cards, Modals, Action Panels:** 16dp (`rounded-xl` equivalent in this system) to establish contained, protective modules that prevent visual clutter.
- **Critical Action Buttons & Pills:** Fully pill-shaped (28dp–32dp radius) on single-action primary targets, or uniform 12dp on segmented status toggles, cleanly signaling interactive clickability.
- **System Badges & Metrics Containers:** 8dp uniform radius, maintaining structural alignment with data tables and connectivity rosters.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Minimum 64px height. Solid `#005F60` fill, white `#FFFFFF` text in 20px bold (`action-primary`), paired with a leading 24px icon. Full-width on mobile. Active states use a deeper `#0B4F50` fill with no motion scaling.
- **Abort / Critical Action:** Minimum 64px height. Solid `#9E2A2B` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, paired with a mandatory `close` or `warning` icon. Reserved strictly for destructive cancellation or stopping distress broadcasts.
- **Secondary / Action Outlined:** 64px height, `#FFFFFF` surface with a 2px solid `#005F60` border, `#005F60` text, and zero background transparency.

### Cards & Telemetry Blocks
- Pure white background (`#FFFFFF`), 16dp rounded corners, and a 1.5px solid `#DDE2DF` boundary border.
- Internal padding is set to `1.25rem` (`20px`).
- Never rely on elevation shadows alone to separate cards; every card must possess structural border definition.

### Status Indicators & Badges
- Triple-verification pattern is mandatory for every state:
  1. **Icon:** Clear system icon (e.g., `check_circle`, `water_drop`, `wifi_tethering`, `battery_full`).
  2. **Text:** Explicit textual label (`CONNECTED MESH`, `WATER RISING`, `PENDING SYNC`).
  3. **Tonal Boundary:** High-contrast background tint with solid border matching the semantic color family.
- Example for Pending Mesh Sync: Background `#FEF3C7`, Border 1.5px `#B36B00`, Text `#B36B00`, Icon `sync_problem`.

### Input Fields
- Minimum height of 64px. Background `#FFFFFF` with 2px continuous border in `#49454F`.
- On focus: 2.5px solid border in `#005F60`.
- Text size: 18px (`body-lg`) with high-contrast `#1A1C1E` placeholder styling (opacity at least 60% for outdoor visibility).

### Checkboxes, Radios, and Switches
- Touch hit target of 64px x 64px with a 28px visible target control.
- Checkbox marks and radio pips use 3px solid strokes in `#005F60` over high-contrast white backgrounds.

### Mesh Peer List Items
- Stacked rows with a minimum height of 72px.
- Incorporates direct human-readable signal strength (`3 hops`, `Direct BT`, `Wi-Fi Direct`) paired with explicit numeric signal or battery telemetry.
- Separated by a 1px solid horizontal rule (`#DDE2DF`).
